JARVIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

JARVIS is a personal AI assistant software project operated by its developer.

Google Data

When connected, JARVIS may use Google APIs such as Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Classroom only with permissions granted by the Google account owner.

Google data is used to provide the requested assistant features. JARVIS does not sell Google user data.

Local Storage

JARVIS is designed as a local-first application. Authentication tokens and application data may be stored locally on the computer running JARVIS.

Contact

For questions about this project, contact the developer through the GitHub repository.
